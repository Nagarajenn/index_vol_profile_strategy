2.1 AI Decision Card — "Should I favor calls, puts, or wait right now?"

The only panel that constitutes the actual trading-decision engine. Four layers by design: Raw Values → AI Interpretation → Confidence → Trading Implication.

MetricLogic
TrendVote of three signals, summed to a −3…+3 score mapped to a 5-level label:
① price vs VWAP (deadband-gated), ② EMA(20) slope over a lookback (deadband-gated), ③ market structure (HH/HL vs LH/LL from fractal swings). analytics/trend_classifier.py
Institutional BiasNear-ATM Call-OI vs Put-OI change + PCR → 5-level label. Live only; returns "Unavailable (historical)" for backfilled rows. analytics/institutional_bias.py
Support / ResistanceCandidate levels (prior-day H/L/C, today's & yesterday's VAH/VAL/POC, round numbers, swing clusters) clustered within a tolerance band into zones; nearest zone above/below price is reported as a range. analytics/support_resistance.py
POC / VWAPSee §2.2 and §2.3.
Confidence 0–100Weighted sum of 7 sub-scores, weights from config/scoring_weights.json: trend alignment 25, VWAP position 15, structure 15, institutional bias 15, trendline confluence 10, S/R proximity 10, breakout confirmation 10. Renormalizes when institutional bias is unavailable. All sub-scores are persisted as real columns so weights can be retuned later without recomputing technicals.
AI InterpretationDeterministic template (not an LLM call) composed from price-vs-VWAP, price-vs-POC, trend label and institutional bias. backend/app/services/interpretation.py
Trading ImplicationDeterministic action text keyed off the confidence/trend/S-R state. Never emits a size or an order.

2.2 Volume Profile Intelligence

MetricLogic
Volume Profile / POC / Value AreaFrom 1-min candles (Dhan gives OHLCV, not tick prints): each candle's volume is distributed across the price bins it spans using 0.5×uniform + 0.5×triangular-centred-on-close, renormalised per candle. Bin size SENSEX 25 / NIFTY 5 points. POC = max-volume bin. Value Area = expand outward from POC, greedily adding the higher adjacent bin until ≥70% of session volume → VAH/VAL. A candle-based approximation of a true tick profile, disclosed as such.
Profile ShapeD / P / b / B classification from the bin distribution around POC (B = two separated HVN clusters = double distribution).
Opening TypeOpen-Drive / Open-Test-Drive / Open-Rejection-Reverse / Open-Auction, from the first 30 minutes' behaviour relative to the opening print.
Initial BalanceHigh/low of the first 60 minutes (DEFAULT_INITIAL_BALANCE_MINUTES).
Rotation FactorNet directional rotation across 30-minute periods — how much the market is rotating vs trending.
Volume PaceCumulative volume vs the elapsed-time-aligned 20-day average. Thresholds 115% / 85% for Above/Below Average.
Value MigrationToday's developing POC/VAH/VAL vs the prior session's, in points and %.
HVN / LVNLocal maxima/minima of the volume-by-price histogram, capped at 5 nodes each.
Acceptance / RejectionPer level, counts candles that traded at the level and closed through vs closed back — "accepted (56)" = 56 accepting touches.

2.4 Volume Intelligence

Foundational proxy: there is no tick data — Dhan provides 1-minute OHLCV only. Buy/sell volume is estimated Chaikin-style: mfm = ((close−low) − (high−close)) / (high−low), buy = volume × max(mfm,0), sell = volume × max(−mfm,0). Note buy + sell ≤ volume; the shortfall is non-directional volume for that bar. This is disclosed in the panel footer itself.
MetricLogic
Relative Volume (RVOL)Current interval & cumulative volume vs elapsed-time-aligned baselines across 6 historical groupings (last 5/10/20 days, same weekday, expiry-day, monthly-expiry). Labels at 115% / 85%. Groups with <3 sample days are omitted rather than shown thin.
Volume Accelerationmean(last 5 candles) / mean(prior 5); ≥1.2 Accelerating, ≤0.8 Decelerating.
Buy/Sell DominanceRolling 8-minute window: buy/(buy+sell); ≥0.55 Buy, ≤0.45 Sell. Plus consecutive same-sign minute streak.
Volume Momentumema(volume × mfm, span 8) normalised by rolling average volume, clipped to [−1,1]. Strong at ≥0.35 with a ≥5-minute streak; ≥0.15 plain.
Institutional Participation 0–100100 × (0.40×rvol + 0.35×blockiness + 0.25×dominance); blockiness = share of candles ≥1.5× the 10-bar mean. Labels Minimal/Low/Moderate/High/Very High at 20/40/60/80. Volume-side only — deliberately does not read OI (that is the Decision Card's institutional bias).
Spike / Dry-upSpike at ≥2.5× baseline; dry-up at ≤0.4×.
Absorption / ExhaustionAbsorption = volume spike with range ≤ 0.6× the prior 10-bar average range. Exhaustion = extended consistent move + volume climax + a rejection wick ≥40% on the move's own side.
Volume Trend20-candle window split 10/10; ±10% → Increasing/Decreasing, ±30% → Strong variants.
Current CharacterFirst-match-wins: Climactic → Accumulation/Distribution (flat price + rising volume + dominance) → Markup/Markdown (directional price + dominance) → Quiet-Consolidation (safe default). Documented as a reasonable heuristic, not a canonical Wyckoff claim.
Significant 5-Min Intervals5-minute buckets whose volume is ≥1.8× the same elapsed-time bucket's historical baseline (needs ≥3 baseline days), annotated with side and whether price moved with or against the flow.
Historical ComparisonPer historical day: distance = 0.5×mean|cumulative-volume-curve Δ| + 0.5×mean|dominance-curve Δ|, similarity = 1/(1+distance). Top 5 shown. Needs ≥5 comparable days and ≥5 elapsed minutes, else honestly empty.
Expected Next BehaviourFixed 10-minute horizon. Weighted composite of 5 components each in [−1,1]: volume trend 0.30, momentum 0.25, character 0.20, institutional 0.15, similarity 0.10 → P(continuation) = clip(0.5 + composite×0.4, 0.1, 0.9) — never fully certain. Confidence drops to Low when <3 components are available.
AI Volume NarrativeDeterministic templates, max 4 observations, each behind its own trigger threshold (e.g. RVOL only speaks when \|RVOL−100\|≥20). Zero observations on a quiet minute is correct behaviour.

2.5 Session AMD

ICT-style single-cycle session model: an opening consolidation (Accumulation), a stop-hunt beyond it that fails and reverses (Manipulation), then the real move (Distribution).

MetricLogic
Accumulation RangeHigh/low of the first 30 minutes (DEFAULT_ACCUMULATION_MINUTES) — reuses compute_initial_balance() with an AMD-specific window, deliberately tighter than Market Profile's 60-min IB.
Sweep (Manipulation)Price breaks the range by ≥SWEEP_MARGIN_PCT = 0.10 × range width, then closes back inside within SWEEP_REVERSAL_WINDOW_MINUTES = 15. Beyond that window without reversing it is classified Breakout (not manipulation) — an honest "this was a real move" read rather than a forced AMD label.
DistributionNet move since the sweep's reversal in the expected (opposite-to-sweep) direction; corroborated by buy/sell dominance since reversal. Status Confirmed / Developing / Failed (price re-crossed the sweep's own extreme).
Current PhaseAccumulating · Range Established–Awaiting Move · Testing Range · Distribution · Breakout (not manipulation) · No Clear Setup (more than one qualifying sweep — reported honestly rather than picking one).

ComponentLogic
Live AdvisorActive only 14:00–15:01 IST. Leakage-safe by construction: cutoff = min(now, PRE_WINDOW_END) — it can never read a candle past its own cutoff. Freezes a snapshot, scores it once, never re-reads post-cutoff candles.
CAS framingNSE's Closing Auction Session from 2026-08-03. Pre-window 14:31–14:59, post-window 15:00–15:39. Volume is only compared 14:31–14:59 vs 15:00–15:14, because Dhan's 1-min feed stops reporting reliable volume once the auction begins at 15:15 — even though price keeps moving genuinely through it.
Transition TypePre-state and post-outcome classified independently — 7 values: CONTINUATION_UP/DOWN, REVERSAL_UP/DOWN, POST_WINDOW_INITIATION_UP/DOWN, NO_MATERIAL_TRANSITION. This fixed a real conceptual bug: a flat pre-window used to swallow a genuine −312 pt post-window move under the label "Neutral".
MagnitudeATR-normalised, not raw points: |post move| / ATR(14) of the prior day, tiered at 0.5 / 1.0 / 2.0 → NORMAL / MODERATE / LARGE / EXTREME. Returns no tier at all when ATR is unavailable rather than fabricating one.
Original engine columnThe pre-redesign 3-way call (continuation/reversal/neutral), kept and shown side-by-side so the redesign is auditable rather than silently replacing history.
Factor Correlation Study40 factors vs the transition outcome. Continuous factors via correlation; categorical via non-parametric tests. Every row carries N and a confidence label; <20 days ⇒ "Insufficient data" for the primary study.
Historical Cohorts7 named cohorts from Transition Type × Magnitude, each compared against the rest of the sample (Mann-Whitney U + rank-biserial effect size) on its 14:55–14:59 pre-3pm state. Separate, lower threshold MIN_N_FOR_COHORT_CONFIDENCE = 5 — with an honest note that a low p-value from a handful of days is not predictive power. Cohorts with N=0 still render, so thin cells are visible rather than hidden.

4. Screen 3 — CAS Day Detail LIVE UI

Route /market-transition-intelligence/cas-day/:symbol/:date. Dedicated full-page view for a single transition day, built because the dual-resolution tables could not fit legibly inside an expandable row.

SectionLogic
3 PM Transition Forecast headlineVerdict + P(UP)/P(DOWN)/P(NO-MOVE), confidence, historical N, similarity, expected-move range, transition risk tier. Verdict priority: INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE → CONFLICTED → NO_CLEAR_EDGE → UP/DOWN/NO_MATERIAL_MOVE. It refuses to make a directional call when evidence does not support one.
"Why?" driversPrimary / secondary / tertiary contributing factors ranked by |contribution|, plus any contradictory factors detected in the 2:59 state vector.
Pre-transition table — FORECAST INFORMATIONSix 5-minute windows covering 14:30–14:59, ~28 fields each: OHLC, net/% change, volume, RVOL, volume acceleration, buy/sell estimate + dominance, VWAP & distance & slope, POC & change & slope, VAH/VAL, PCR & change, call/put OI change, IV change, option pressure, market regime, institutional bias.
Post-transition table — ACTUAL OUTCOMESixteen native 1-minute rows 15:00–15:15, plus a single 15:30 closing-print checkpoint (added because real, tradeable movement happens at the 3:30 close after the 3:15 session end). Each row carries a 0–100 Transition Shock Score: 0.25×ATR-normalised move + 0.25×volume surge + 0.20×range expansion + 0.15×buy/sell imbalance + 0.15×option pressure, with the option term reweighted to zero (never fabricated) when option data is missing.
Visual separationThe two tables are separated by a distinctly styled "3 PM TRANSITION" divider so forecast information and actual outcome can never be misread as one continuous table.
Forecast vs Actual stripThe frozen 14:59 forecast's probabilities against what the day actually did.
Why dual resolution: pre-3pm is about the market evolving toward the decision, which 5-minute windows summarise cleanly; post-3pm is about how the transition actually manifests, which needs native 1-minute granularity. This was an explicit correction to an earlier uniform-resolution design.

The honest state of the research: there is no demonstrated, out-of-sample directional edge, and no demonstrated way to express one profitably in options after real ask/bid costs. Test accuracy exactly equalled the majority-class floor. Every candidate improvement collapsed under scrutiny of sample size. This is the correct and useful finding to carry forward — not a reason to keep searching for a formula that looks better.

7. What was removed, and why

Market Intelligence (news & sentiment) has been removed from the UI in this change: the summary bar on the Terminal, the /market-intelligence route, and the top-nav link. The slot it occupied is reserved for the paper-trading UI arriving in the coming milestones.

8. Next phase — paper trading

What the next phase inherits from this one:

AssetState
Live pipeline & data capture✅ Stable, 1-min, both symbols, no changes needed
Leakage-safe research harness✅ Frozen split, Training-only fitting, truncate-vs-extended leakage tests
Realistic option execution model✅ Ask-entry / bid-exit, MFE/MAE, spread & theta accounting (pre-cost)
NO-TRADE as a first-class outcome⚠️ Implemented, but its gates never bound on this sample — needs recalibration
A validated directional edge❌ Does not exist yet
A validated option-expression rule❌ Does not exist yet
UI slot for paper trading✅ Freed by the Market Intelligence removal
Carry this constraint forward: a paper-trading agent built now would be paper-trading a signal that has not demonstrated an edge out-of-sample, and an option-expression rule that lost to not trading on median in all three tiers. That is legitimate as an instrumented research experiment — it keeps collecting data and validates the plumbing end-to-end — but it must never be presented, in the UI or anywhere else, as evidence that the strategy is profitable. The single most valuable input remains more trading days: the sample needs roughly 90–120 option-covered symbol-days before an expanding-window walk-forward can carry real confidence, against 46 today.
