Source of the decisions. 12C does not persist its decisions: the panel computes them per request and stores nothing. The engine is deterministic, so the reconstruction reproduces what was on screen, but it is a reconstruction, not a log of it.
Execution model. A decision at minute m uses only data up to m; that snapshot reaches the database about 15–30 s after m, so the earliest executable quote is m+1. Entries are the ASK at m+1, exits the BID at the minute after the exit signal. No LTP, no forward-fill, no interpolation, no chosen exits. 12C stores no executable strike, so the strike is the ATM resolved at the signal minute — labelled COUNTERFACTUAL ATM PROXY throughout. Gross, before brokerage, STT, exchange charges and slippage.

Data limitations

  • 12C persists nothing, so this is a deterministic reconstruction, not a recording of the live panel.
  • No executable strike is stored by 12C; every trade uses the ATM at the signal minute (COUNTERFACTUAL ATM PROXY).
  • Option data is one snapshot per minute, so entries and exits can only be placed on minute boundaries; a real fill inside the minute would differ.
  • Quotes are top-of-book bid/ask for one lot; depth, partial fills and market impact are not modelled.
  • Gross only — brokerage, STT, exchange charges, GST and slippage are excluded, and they are material at this trade count.
  • One session. Nothing here is evidence about the strategy in general.
